(medium) A Bayesian linear model has posterior sd of the slope 0.177 and of the intercept 0.333. Why does the predictive sd at x = 6 exceed that at x = 0? Answer: the slope error is multiplied by x, so it contributes 6 × 0.177 = 1.06 at x = 6 and nothing at x = 0 (ignoring the correlation between the two weights, which reduces it somewhat).
